## 👋 About Me

I'm a former high-frequency trading quant turned entrepreneur and the **Co-Founder & CEO of AllMind**, where we're building the next generation of AI-powered investment research infrastructure.

My background spans quantitative finance, artificial intelligence, and software engineering.

I started AllMind to solve a problem I saw firsthand in institutional finance: **the world's financial knowledge is fragmented across thousands of disconnected sources.**

Investment professionals spend countless hours gathering information from financial terminals, company filings, research reports, market data providers, and internal systems.

We're building the infrastructure to connect it all.

## 🧠 What We're Building at AllMind

**AllMind is an AI-powered financial research platform designed for institutional investors**, including hedge funds, asset managers, sell-side research teams, and corporate finance professionals.

Our mission is simple: **make investment research suck less.**

We're developing a unified research system built around four core capabilities:

### 🌐 1. Unified Financial Data

Our [financial data infrastructure](https://allmind.ai/platform/data) connects more than **7,000 premium financial and alternative data sources** with an institution's proprietary research.

This includes company filings, financial statements, earnings transcripts, market data, analyst estimates, broker research, expert insights, and alternative datasets.

### 🔗 2. Financial Ontology & Knowledge Graphs

Our [financial ontology](https://allmind.ai/platform/ontology) connects relationships between companies, people, products, suppliers, events, and financial metrics.

Instead of treating documents and datasets as disconnected information, we build a relationship layer that allows AI to understand financial context and uncover connections that traditional search tools can miss.

### 🤖 3. AI Agents for Investment Research

We're developing autonomous AI agents capable of conducting multi-step financial research, analyzing companies, investigating investment theses, and synthesizing evidence from multiple sources.

The objective is to move beyond basic AI chatbots toward AI systems that can execute complex research workflows.

### 📊 4. Investment Research Automation

AllMind helps investment teams automate recurring research workflows, including:

- Earnings previews, analysis, and reports
- Equity research and company deep dives
- Portfolio monitoring and investment thesis tracking
- Financial modeling and comparable-company analysis
- Market intelligence and competitive research
- Due diligence and investment memo preparation

Our goal is to help investment professionals spend less time assembling information and more time making informed decisions.
